Market Terminology
Share / Stock#1
A unit of ownership in a company. Buying a share makes you a partial owner.
EX Buying 1 share of Reliance for ₹2,500.
Sensex & Nifty#2
Benchmark indices representing the market health (Top 30 BSE & Top 50 NSE companies).
EX Nifty up 1% means top 50 companies did well.
Bull vs Bear#3
Bull: Market rising, optimism high. Bear: Market falling (>20%), pessimism high.
EX "Bull run" means sustained price increase.
IPO#4
Initial Public Offering. First time a private company sells shares to the public.
EX Zomato listing on the exchange in 2021.
Demat Account#1
An account to hold shares in electronic format, mandatory for trading in India.
EX Like a bank account for your shares.
Intraday#2
Buying and selling shares on the same day. No delivery to Demat account.
EX Buy Tata Motors at 10 AM, sell at 3 PM.
Stop Loss#3
An automatic order to sell a stock if it falls to a certain price to limit loss.
EX Buy at 100, Stop Loss at 95.
P/E Ratio#1
Price-to-Earnings. How much you pay for ₹1 of profit. High P/E = Expensive/Growth.
EX P/E 20 means paying ₹20 for ₹1 profit.
EPS#2
Earnings Per Share. Net Profit divided by total shares. Higher is better.
EX Profit 100Cr, 10Cr shares -> EPS = ₹10.
ROE#3
Return on Equity. Measures profitability relative to shareholder money.
EX ROE 15% means ₹15 profit on every ₹100 invested.
